The Mission

What is WorkAware?

The WorkAware Campaign is a digital initiative by AIESEC Future Leaders to increase labor rights literacy among youth and students through engaging Instagram content. Campaign materials include carousels, infographics, short videos, and interactive features such as quizzes and Q&A sessions.

Instagram Campaign

Reels, carousels, infographics, and Story interaction.

Youth Education

Basic labor rights, fair wages, safe work, and contracts.

Why This Campaign?

Limited Knowledge

Many young people begin working with very little understanding of wages, hours, or safety regulations, which increases the risk of exploitation.

Unfair Treatment

Without knowing their rights, youth may accept low pay, excessive working hours, or unsafe workplaces as normal conditions.

Preventing Violations

Accessible and clear education on labor rights helps young workers identify red flags early and take action to protect themselves.

Implementation Roadmap

Pre-Production

Conduct research on labor rights, identify common violations, and prepare visual concepts using tools such as Canva or Figma.

1
Focus
Research & Design

Content Production

Produce 5–7 pieces of content, including Labor Rights 101, signs of violations, legal working hours, wages, and reporting channels.

2
Focus
Creation & Editing

Posting & Engagement

Schedule uploads 1–2 times per week and use Story quizzes, polls, and Q&A sessions to build interaction.

3
Focus
Interaction & Reach

Monitoring

Track Instagram insights (reach, engagement, saves), collect feedback, and summarize results in a final report.

4
Focus
Evaluation

Program Schedule (1 Month)

WEEK 1

WEEK 2

Launch

WEEK 3

Momentum

  • Post second and third content batch

  • Share short questionnaire

WEEK 4

Closing

  • Evaluate insights and feedback
  • Prepare final campaign report
